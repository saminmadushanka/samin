About Us

Our story

PROPACE Consultancy was founded in Fiji to give organisations of every size access to world-class project delivery expertise. We believe good project management is a practical discipline: clear scope, honest planning, engaged people and steady governance.

Our missionTo help organisations deliver change successfully and to build the project capability of the people who deliver it.
